A suggestive random list of possibly possible essay questions to be asked on your exams

Introduction to Psychology

  1. What is the definition of psychology and what type of science is it?

  2. How is psychology different from pop pyschology?

  3. Regarding the early schools of Psychology, what did Structuralism and Functionalism study and what method did they use?

  4. What are the origins of psychology? Please explain all history in detail.

  5. Who was Wilhelm Wundt?

  6. Who was William James?

  7. Who was Sigmund Freud?

  8. What is Empiricism? How is it related to psychology?

  9. Who was Ernst Weber?

  10. Who was Gustav Fechner?

  11. Who was Hermann von Helmholtz?

  12. Describe Stucturalism, Functionalism, and Behaviorism.

  13. What is the difference between Humanistic and Positivie psychology?

  14. What is Cognitivism?

  15. What is Gestalt psychology?

  16. Who was John Locke?

  17. Define Psychology and its Subdisciplines.

  18. Who was John B. Watson?

  19. Who was B. F. Skinner?

  20. What is Behavioral Genetics, Behavioral Neuroscience, and Evolutionary Psychology?

  21. Who was G. Stanley Hall?

  22. Describe humanistic and positive psychology and name their main representatives. How did the two fields emerge and develop?

  23. What is the definition of evolution?

  24. Describe how natural selection works in 4 steps by giving an example.

  25. Please list and describe all the different psychological perspectives that are used to describe human behavior, what is the focus and primary assumptions for each, and who were the key figures?

  26. Compare and contrast the main purposes and goals of educational, developmental, and personality psychology study.

  27. What is critical thinking and what are its six activities?

  28. What is metacognitive thinking?

Conducting Research in Psychology

  1. What is the Nature of Science, what does its attitude comprise of and what is the scientific method?

  2. What is meta-analysis?

  3. What is Big Data?

  4. Is it possible for researchers the outcome of their study and if true what is done in order to lessen this phenomenon?
  5. Describe Descriptive and inferential statistics, and variability?

  6. What are the different Research Designs in Psychology and describe them?

  7. What do you know about Reserach Ethics?

  8. What are the main characteristics of correlational designs? Name the three most common kinds of descriptive methods and describe them.

  9. What are the main characteristics of descriptive designs? Name the three most common kinds of descriptive methods and describe them.

  10. What are the main characteristics of experimental designs? Name the three most common kinds of descriptive methods and describe them.

  11. Who is Rosenthal, what is experimenter expectancy and self-fulfilling prophecy? What do you know about the aforementioned?

  12. What are the commonly used measures in psychology? Describe them, their uses, and their limitations

  13. What do you know about Ethical Treatment and what are is the guideline that research must adhere to?

  14. What do you know about the Milgram’s studies of obedience

  2. What do you know about Coping?

Personality: The Uniqueness of the Individual

  2. What are the different perspectives on Personality?

  3. What are the origins of Personality?

  4. How is Personality measured?

Social Behavior

  1. What types of conformity do you know and describe them.

  2. What was the point of the Milgram's study? What do you know about obedience?

  3. What do you know about attributions? What is self-serving bias and fundamental attribution error?

  4. What are schemas? What are stereotypes?

  5. What is Social Perception?

  6. Define attributions. Explain the concepts of (a) self-serving bias and (b) fundamental attribution error. Provide an example of each bias/error.

  7. Define attitude and describe its diffrent components.

  8. What is cognitive dissonance?

  9. What is persuasion and what makes it successful?

  10. What is prejudice?

  11. What do you know about Social Relations?

  12. What is agression and what are its different types and what makes us aggresive?

  13. What is the Social Learning Theory and by whom?

  14. Desribe Prosocial behavior.

  15. What is the bystander effect?

  16. Analyze altruism and its different explanations.

  17. Can there be true altruism?

  18. What is empathy?

  19. What are the motivations to help others? What is compassion?

  20. What is assortative mating?

  21. What faces do people like most?

  22. What does average mean in attractiveness?

  23. Does scent play a role in attractiveness?

  24. What is the sexual strategies theory?

  25. Can you define love? What types of love are there? What is the triangular theory of love and by whom?
