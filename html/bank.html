A suggestive random list of possibly possible essay questions to be asked on your exams

Introduction to Psychology

  1. Regarding the early schools of Psychology, what did Structuralism and Functionalism study and what method did they use?

  2. Describe Stucturalism, Functionalism, and Behaviorism.

  3. Define Psychology and its Subdisciplines.

  4. Describe humanistic and positive psychology and name their main representatives. How did the two fields emerge and develop?

  5. Describe how natural selection works.

  6. Compare and contrast the main purposes and goals of educational, developmental, and personality psychology study.

Conducting Research in Psychology

  1. What is the Nature of Science, what does its attitude comprise of and what is the scientific method?

  2. What is meta-analysis?

  3. What is Big Data?

  4. Is it possible for researchers the outcome of their study and if true what is done in order to lessen this phenomenon?
  5. Describe Descriptive and inferential statistics, and variability?

  6. What are the different Research Designs in Psychology and describe them?

  7. What do you know about Reserach Ethics?

  8. What are the main characteristics of correlational designs? Name the three most common kinds of descriptive methods and describe ONE of them.

  9. What are the main characteristics of descriptive designs? Name the three most common kinds of descriptive methods and describe ONE of them.

  10. What are the main characteristics of experimental designs? Name the three most common kinds of descriptive methods and describe ONE of them.

  11. Who is Rosenthal, what is experimenter expectancy and self-fulfilling prophecy? What do you know about the aforementioned?

  12. What are the commonly used measures in psychology? Describe them, their uses, and their limitations

  13. What do you know about Ethical Treatment and what are is the guideline that research must adhere to?

  14. What do you know about the Milgram’s studies of obedience

Human Development

  1. What do you know about the developing fetus?

  2. What do you know about the developing infant and child?

  3. What do you know about the developing adolescent?

  4. What do you know about the developing adult?

Intelligence, Problem Solving, and Creativity

  1. Define Intelligence

  2. What do you know about Problem Solving?

  3. What do you know about Creativity?

Stress and Health

  1. Define Stress

  2. What do you know about Coping?

  3. How does stress and coping affect health?

Personality: The Uniqueness of the Individual

  1. Define Personality.

  2. What are the different perspectives on Personality?

  3. What are the origins of Personality?

  4. How is Personality measured?

Social Behavior

  1. What is Social Perception?

  2. Define attributions. Explain the concepts of (a) self-serving bias and (b) fundamental attribution error. Provide an example of each bias/error.

  3. What do you know about Attitudes and Behavior?

  4. What do you know about Social Relations?

Psychological Disorders

  1. Define what is a Psychological Disorder?

  2. What are Neurodevelopmental Disorders?

  3. List and describe the main disorders referenced in your book.

Treatment of Psychological Disorders

  1. What do you know about Biomedical Treatment for Psychological Disorders?

  2. What do you know about Psychological Treatment for Psychological Disorders?

  3. What do you know about Technology-based Treatment for Psychological Disorders?
